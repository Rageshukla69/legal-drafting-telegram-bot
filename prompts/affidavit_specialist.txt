2. **Case Number & Parties (`case_heading` and `parties`)**:
   - For **Administrative, University, Scholarship, Bank, Tehsil, or General Notary Affidavits** where there is NO lawsuit between a Plaintiff (`वादी`) and Defendant (`प्रतिवादी`), leave `case_heading` as `""` and `parties` as `[]` (or only the Subject line `"विषय : ..."` if appropriate). 3. **Title (`title`)**:
   - Write a clear, formal centered title such as `"शपथ-पत्र (AFFIDAVIT)"` or `"शपथ-पत्र बाबत [उद्देश्य/विषय]"`.
4. **Deponent Introduction (`opening_averment`)**:
