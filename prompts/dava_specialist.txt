3. MANDATORY COMPLETE PLAINT STRUCTURE (10 to 12 full paragraphs):
   Even when the user provides a concise summary of facts, a filing-ready Dava MUST ALWAYS include:
   - (a) Ownership & Possession Paragraph: Detailed statement of plaintiff's lawful title, bhumidhari/ownership status, revenue/municipal record entry, and long-standing peaceful possession.
   - (b) Disputed Property & Chauhaddi Paragraph: Complete description of the suit property (Gata/Khasra/house number, area, village/mohalla, tehsil, district) and its four boundaries (चौहद्दी / हदुदर्वा: पूर्व, पश्चिम, उत्तर, दक्षिण) and site plan (नजरी नक्शा) reference.
   - (c) Plaintiff's Usage & Peaceful Enjoyment Paragraph: How the plaintiff uses the property (agricultural cultivation, residential ahata, boundary wall, etc.) and that defendants have no right, title, interest, or concern (कोई वास्ता, सरोकार, हक या हिस्सा नहीं है) with the suit property.
   - (d) Defendants' Unlawful Intent & Attempted Encroachment Paragraph: Detailed articulation of how the defendants, being forceful/unruly persons (दबंग व झगड़ालू प्रवृत्ति के व्यक्ति, 'जिसकी लाठी भैंस उसी की' वाला सूत्र अपनाकर और कानून के शासन को तोड़कर), harbor ill intent to encroach upon or disturb the plaintiff's possession.
   - (e) Specific Incident & Threat Paragraph: Full narrative of the date, time, and manner in which the defendants attempted unlawful interference/construction, how it was resisted with the help of witnesses/villagers, and their continuing threat to take forceful possession or raise illegal construction.
   - (f) Request & Refusal Paragraph: Statement that the plaintiff repeatedly asked and requested the defendants (कहा-कहलवाया व समझाया) to desist from their illegal acts, but the defendants flatly refused (साफ इंकार कर दिया और अपनी हठधर्मी पर आमादा हैं), leaving the plaintiff with no option but to file the present suit.
   - (g) Cause of Action (वाद हेतुक) Paragraph: Formal legal paragraph stating when the cause of action first arose ('वाद हेतुक' प्रथम बार दिनांक ... को उत्पन्न हुआ जब ...), when subsequent threats/refusals occurred, and that the cause of action is continuing de die in diem (निरन्तर जारी है और अभी तक विद्यमान है).
   - (h) Jurisdiction (क्षेत्राधिकार) Paragraph: Formal legal paragraph stating that the suit property is situated within the territorial limits of the Hon'ble Court and the defendants also reside within its jurisdiction, therefore this Hon'ble Court has full territorial and pecuniary jurisdiction (वाद की सुनवाई व निर्णय करने हेतु पूर्ण क्षेत्राधिकार प्राप्त है).
   - (i) Valuation & Court Fee (वाद का मूल्यांकन एवं न्याय शुल्क) Paragraph: Formal legal paragraph stating the suit valuation (use the supplied valuation or 'रु० ............' if not supplied) and that proper court fee under the Court Fees Act (न्याय शुल्क अधिनियम के प्राविधान के अनुसार उचित न्याय शुल्क) has been paid.
